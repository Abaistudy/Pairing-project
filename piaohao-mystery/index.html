兩百年前的加密簽名

A cryptographic signature, two centuries early

票號 · 1844

  • 🧠 Secret rhyme memorized by apprentices — 「謹防假票冒取勿忘細視書章」 = the key
  • ✍️ Cipher characters binding date + sum — the MAC / signature
  • 🏦 Any branch, no telegraph needed — distributed verification
  • 🔁 Rhymes rotated every few years — key rotation

Internet · Today

  • 🔑 Shared secret key known only to sender & receiver
  • 🔏 HMAC / digital signature binds message content
  • 🌍 Any server verifies without calling home
  • ⏳ Keys expire and are replaced on schedule

"Hui tong tian xia — remittances flow across the empire." Shanxi merchants ran a trust network over 51 cities with paper, ink, and a 56-character rhyme — a handwritten message-authentication code, two hundred years before digital signatures existed.

Historical basis: cipher rhymes of Rishengchang 日昇昌 (est. 1823, Pingyao, Shanxi), the first Chinese draft bank (piaohao).
